# 75Li₂S–25P₂S₅ solid electrolyte by mechanical milling

All weighing, mixing, loading and recovery is done **in the glovebox**. The milling itself uses the [Pulverisette 7](../instruments/ball-mill.md) with the sealed ZrO₂ pot in its steel tank — watch the [ball-mill demonstration](../instruments/ball-mill.md#demonstration) first.

## Recipe (2 g batch)

| Reagent | Mass | Molar ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Li₂S | 0.7657 g | 75 |
| P₂S₅ | 1.2346 g | 25 |

<small>Check: with M(Li₂S) = 45.95 g mol⁻¹ and M(P₂S₅) = 222.27 g mol⁻¹, a 75:25 molar mixture is 38.3 wt % Li₂S, i.e. 0.766 g Li₂S + 1.234 g P₂S₅ per 2 g. The listed masses agree.</small>

## Procedure

1. **Hand-mix.** Grind Li₂S and P₂S₅ gently in an agate mortar for **30 min** until you have a uniform, soft, pale-yellow powder.
2. **Load.** Transfer **1 g** of the mixture into the ZrO₂ pot with **90 g of Ø 5 mm** milling balls (ball-to-powder ratio 90:1). Close with the white polymer gasket and ZrO₂ lid.
3. **Seal.** Close the pot inside the stainless-steel tank with a PTFE spacer above and below, and seal with the rubber gasket.
4. **Mill** at **510 rpm for 24 h** (see the duty-cycle warning below).
5. **First recovery.** In the glovebox, move all balls to a separate container and scrape as much powder as possible out of the pot with a spatula into another container.
6. **Basket recovery.** Put the nickel basket in the pot, return all the balls into it, close the pot in the tank as before and mill at **210 rpm for 30 min**.
7. **Collect.** Back in the glovebox, put the balls aside, carefully remove the nickel basket and collect the released powder with a spatula.
8. **Repeat** steps 6–7 at least **3–4 times** until no more powder is recovered.
9. **Store** all the powder in an argon-filled glass vial labelled **"75Li₂S–25P₂S₅ BM"** and the date.

!!! warning "Before you run it"
    - **Duty cycle.** 24 h of continuous milling exceeds the manufacturer's rating for this mill (30 % duty cycle, 8 h shifts). See the [ball-mill page](../instruments/ball-mill.md#demonstration) and agree a milling/pause program with your supervisor.
    - **Balance.** The steel tank is heavy: the opposite station needs a bowl of the same mass.
    - **H₂S.** Li₂S and P₂S₅ release H₂S with moisture. Keep everything in the glovebox and close containers immediately (the glovebox catalyst is poisoned by sulfur compounds).

!!! note "Record for every batch"
    Batch masses, ball mass and size, rpm, milling and pause times, number of recovery cycles, final yield (g and %), glovebox O₂/H₂O readings. Yield is lost mainly to the pot walls and balls, so the recovery steps matter for comparing batches.
