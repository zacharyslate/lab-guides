# Cathode composite by the solution (rotavapor) route

The electrolyte (Li₆PS₅Cl, LPSC) is dissolved in ethanol and re-precipitated onto NMC and carbon fibre while the solvent is removed on the [rotavapor](../instruments/rotavapor.md). Steps 1–3 and 6–7 are done **in the glovebox**.

## Recipe

| Component | Mass | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Li₆PS₅Cl | 180 mg | solid electrolyte |
| NMC-111 | 114 mg | active material |
| VGCF | 6 mg | carbon fibre (electronic conduction) |
| Ethanol | 10 mL | dissolves LPSC |
| Acetonitrile | 10 mL | added before drying |

<small>Composition of the dry composite: 60 wt % LPSC, 38 wt % NMC-111, 2 wt % VGCF (300 mg total).</small>

## Procedure

1. **Prepare two bottles.** Bottle 1: LPSC + 10 mL ethanol. Bottle 2: VGCF + NMC-111, no solvent.
2. **Dissolve** all the LPSC in the ethanol (bottle 1), then transfer the **entire contents** of bottle 1 into bottle 2.
3. **Mix.** Add 10 mL of acetonitrile and mix for **2 min** on the vortex mixer in the glovebox.
4. **Transfer** everything to a round-bottom flask and close it with the valve adapter and Keck clip, valve closed, as shown in [Part 1 of the rotavapor guide](../instruments/rotavapor.md#part-1-assemble-the-flask-in-the-glovebox). Take the closed flask out of the glovebox.
5. **Dry on the rotavapor** following [Part 2 of the rotavapor guide](../instruments/rotavapor.md#part-2-on-the-rotavapor) — cool under vacuum and close the stopcock before venting.
6. **Collect** all the dry powder from the flask (back in the glovebox) and grind it in a mortar and pestle until it is uniform and soft.
7. **Store** the powder in a bottle labelled **"NMC Cathode"** with the NMC wt % (38 %) and the date.

!!! note "Record the drying conditions"
    Bath temperature, set pressure and drying time are not fixed yet for this procedure. Write down the values you use, and any heat treatment after drying, so the run can be reproduced.

!!! warning "Solvent safety"
    Ethanol and acetonitrile are flammable; acetonitrile is toxic. Keep volumes small in the glovebox, keep bottles capped, and see the note on solvents in the [glovebox guide](../instruments/glovebox.md#things-that-damage-the-box).
