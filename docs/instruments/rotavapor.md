# Büchi Rotavapor R-210

<div class="facts" markdown>

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is for** | Removing solvent under reduced pressure, e.g. drying the cathode composite suspension (ethanol + acetonitrile) |
| **Parts** | **Büchi Recirculating Chiller F-105** (condenser coolant) · **Nahita Digital Oil Bath** filled with **silicone oil** · **R-210** with power switch, **ROTATION** knob (0–10), tilt knob, Combi Clip and motorised lift · vertical condenser and receiving flask · **Büchi Vacuum Controller V-850** on **Vacuum Pump V-700** |
| **Flask** | For air-sensitive work: a round-bottom flask closed with a **valve adapter (glass stopcock)** and a **Keck clip**, assembled and closed in the glovebox (Part 1 below) |
| **Training** | Required. Pass the rotavapor exam before booking |
| **Manuals** | [Manuals and references](../manuals.md#rotavapor) |

</div>

!!! danger "Safety"
    - Lab coat, safety glasses, gloves and closed shoes.
    - The silicone oil bath can reach **180 °C**. Never add water or any chemical to the oil bath: water in hot oil causes violent splashing (Büchi manual, §2).
    - Silicone oil expands a lot when heated. A bath that looks half full when cold is **not** low — do not top it up.
    - Inspect every glass part for cracks or star marks before use: damaged glass can **implode** under vacuum. Use the clips on every joint.

## Part 1 — Assemble the flask in the glovebox

The sample never sees air: the flask is filled, closed with the valve adapter and clipped **inside the glovebox**, and opened again only under vacuum on the rotavapor.

<div class="demo" data-demo="rv-glassware">
--8<-- "rv-glassware.svg"
</div>

<div class="demo-steps" markdown>

1. **Gather the parts in the glovebox:** the round-bottom flask with your suspension (standing in a cork ring), the **valve adapter** with its glass stopcock, **vacuum grease** and a **Keck clip** that fits the joint size.
2. **Inspect the glass.** Joints must be clean and free of chips; the flask must have no cracks or star marks — it will be under vacuum.
3. **Grease the joint.** Put a **thin band** of vacuum grease on the upper part of the adapter's lower (male) joint only. Too much grease creeps into the flask and contaminates the sample.
4. **Insert and turn.** Push the adapter into the flask neck and turn it gently until the greased band looks clear and continuous, with no streaks or air channels.
5. **Clip the joint.** Put the **Keck clip** over the joint so the adapter cannot slip out.
6. **Close the valve.** Turn the stopcock handle to **horizontal = closed** (vertical = open).
7. **Final check:** clip on, valve horizontal, flask labelled. Keep the upper joint (the one that goes onto the rotavapor) clean.
8. **Take it out** through the antechamber ([glovebox guide](glovebox.md#taking-material-out)) and carry it to the rotavapor holding the flask by its body. **Keep the valve closed** until step 9 of Part 2.

</div>

## Part 2 — On the rotavapor

<div class="demo" data-demo="rotavapor">
--8<-- "rotavapor.svg"
</div>

<div class="demo-steps" markdown>

1. **Safety check.** Put on your PPE, look at the oil level and check the glassware for damage. The bath must contain only silicone oil.
2. **Switch the chiller on — power only.** Press the green power switch on the F-105: the display lights up with SET TEMP and ACTUAL TEMP. Do **not** press START yet.
3. **Set the oil bath.** Switch the Nahita bath on (POWER). Press **SET**, change the set value (**SV**, green) with ◄ ▼ ▲ and press **SET** again. **PV** (red) is the actual oil temperature.
4. **Set the pressure** on the V-850 vacuum controller (Manual mode): press the **Set** soft key, turn the black knob to the pressure your procedure asks for and confirm. The set value is shown at the top of the display ("Set … mbar"); the large number is the actual pressure.
5. **Fit the receiving flask.** Put a thin film of vacuum grease on its joint and attach it under the condenser with its ball-joint clip.
6. **Fit the flask from Part 1.** Check the stopcock is still **horizontal (closed)**. Press the lock button on the drive, push the adapter's upper joint onto the vapour duct (it needs very little force) and fix it with the **Combi Clip**: press the clamp down and turn it **clockwise**. Support the flask with one hand until it is fixed.
7. **Start the vacuum.** Check that the vacuum pump V-700 is switched on (switch at its front), check the set pressure and press **START** on the controller. The closed flask is not yet exposed to vacuum.
8. **Start the chiller refrigeration** after the vacuum has run for about 5 min: press **START** on the F-105. A snowflake appears on its display and ACTUAL TEMP falls towards SET TEMP.
9. **Open the flask and start rotating.** When the chiller has been cooling for at least 5 min, turn the stopcock **slowly** towards open (handle in line with the adapter) — the pressure rises briefly. Switch the rotavapor on (green switch on its top panel) and turn the **ROTATION** knob up to the speed you need.
10. **Adjust the position.** The black knob on the drive sets the tilt of the flask; the handle with the electric switch moves the whole glass assembly up and down. The safety catch at the bottom of the lift stops the flask from going too low.
11. **Lower the flask slowly** into the oil bath.
12. **Evaporate.** Solvent condenses in the condenser and collects in the receiving flask. Watch for bumping; if it bumps, lift the flask out of the bath and raise the set pressure.
13. **Finish without letting air in.** With the vacuum still on and the stopcock open, stop the rotation (**ROTATION** knob to 0), raise the flask out of the bath and let it cool under vacuum. Only then **close the stopcock** (handle across the adapter).
14. **Stop and vent.** Press **STOP once** to stop the vacuum, **twice** to vent the system. Press **STOP** on the chiller and switch the oil bath off. Hold the flask and release it by turning the **Combi Clip anticlockwise**; take the closed flask (adapter and Keck clip still on) back into the glovebox before opening it. Empty the receiving flask into the correct solvent-waste container.

</div>

!!! warning "Air-sensitive samples"
    Venting (STOP pressed twice) lets **air** into the glass assembly. Your sample is protected only if the flask stopcock is **closed before** you vent. Never vent with the stopcock open.

!!! tip "Wait for the chiller"
    Five minutes is a minimum. If ACTUAL TEMP on the chiller is still well above SET TEMP when you open the flask, the solvent is not condensed and goes to the pump.
