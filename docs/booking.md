# Book an instrument

Choose the instrument (and channel, for potentiostats and cyclers), look at the week, then fill in the form. Click an empty hour in the calendar to pre-fill the start time. Bookings can span several days (ball mill, furnace, cyclers).

<div id="booking-app" class="bk"></div>

## How booking works

1. You request a booking with your **institutional e-mail**.
2. You receive an e-mail with a **confirmation** button. The booking is held as *(pending)* for 30 minutes and released if you do not confirm.
3. The same e-mail has a **cancel** button. Please cancel bookings you will not use.

## Rules

- Book only instruments you have been **trained** on.
- Arrive on time; a booking not started within 30 min may be used by someone else *(lab rule to be confirmed)*.
- Long runs (cyclers, ball mill, furnace) must be booked for their **whole duration**, including pauses and cooling.
- Report faults to the lab manager immediately and note them in the instrument logbook.

!!! info "Privacy"
    The calendar shows only your **initials and group**. Your full name, e-mail and purpose are visible to the lab manager only.
