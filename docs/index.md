## How to use the demonstrations

Each guide has an animation of the instrument and the cell or vessel it uses. Press **▶ Play** to watch it, use **◀ / ▶** to go step by step, or click any step in the written list under the animation. The written steps and the animation are the same list, so you can follow either.

## Before you use any instrument

1. Read the [safety rules](safety.md) and the guide for the instrument.
2. Get **hands-on training** from the lab manager and pass the instrument exam where there is one.
3. **Book** the instrument.
4. Keep a record in your lab notebook: date, instrument, settings, sample, and anything unusual.
