# Safety first

These rules apply to every instrument. Each guide adds the specific hazards of that instrument.

## Personal protective equipment

- Lab coat, safety glasses, closed shoes and long trousers — always.
- Nitrile gloves for chemicals; in the glovebox, **nitrile gloves over the butyl box gloves**.
- Heat-resistant gloves and a face shield for furnaces, ovens and hot milling bowls.
- Laser-protection glasses for the Raman spectrometer when the beam is not enclosed.

## Hazards specific to our materials

| Hazard | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **H₂S** from sulfides (Li₂S, P₂S₅, Li₃PS₄, Li₆PS₅Cl) with moisture | Glovebox, milling pots, waste | Handle sulfides only under argon. Open used pots and vials in the glovebox. Never put sulfides in the oven or the open-air furnace. Quench sulfide waste only as the lab procedure describes |
| **Lithium metal** | Glovebox, battery assembly | Keep away from water. Use only cut, clean pieces. Dispose of Li scraps as the lab procedure describes |
| **Flammable / toxic solvents** (ethanol, acetonitrile, THF) | Glovebox, rotavapor | Small volumes, capped bottles. Never into the drying oven |
| **Vacuum and pressure** | Antechamber, rotavapor, Monowave vials, hydraulic press | Inspect glass for cracks; never evacuate closed solvent bottles fully; respect vial limits |
| **Laser class 3B** | Cora 5001, Monowave 400 R probe | Disarm before moving the probe; never look into the probe or fibre |
| **Hot surfaces** | Oil bath (≤ 180 °C), furnace (≤ 1100 °C lab limit), milling bowls (≤ 110 °C) | Gloves, tongs, let things cool |
| **Moving parts** | Ball mill, rotavapor lift | Hood closed; fingers clear of clamps and lifts |

## If something goes wrong

- **Spill or broken glass in the glovebox:** stop, keep the doors closed, tell the lab manager.
- **High O₂/H₂O in the glovebox:** close all sensitive containers and tell the lab manager. Do not regenerate yourself.
- **Smell of rotten eggs (H₂S):** leave the area, warn others, and call the lab manager.
- **Fire, injury or exposure:** follow the institute emergency procedure posted in the lab.
